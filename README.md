## 🔍 What Is This?

A free tool to help citizens, journalists, and researchers investigate Minnesota's massive fraud scandal — estimated at **$9 billion or more** across multiple programs:

- **Feeding Our Future** — $250M+ stolen, 78 indicted
- **CCAP Daycare Fraud** — $1B+ estimated, 62 investigations  
- **EIDBI Autism Services** — $220M+ in fraud
- **Housing Stabilization** — $302M, program terminated

---

## 🚀 How To Use It

### Step 1: Search
Type any name, organization, or program into the search box. Examples:
- `Feeding Our Future`
- `Tim Walz`
- `Quality Learning Center`

### Step 2: View Results
The site searches **real government databases**:
- Federal grants & contracts (USASpending.gov)
- Nonprofit tax filings (ProPublica)
- Campaign finance records (FEC.gov)

### Step 3: Deep Research + AI Analysis
Click **"🔍 Deep Research + AI"** to:
1. Get links to search news sites, DOJ records, and courts
2. Copy your research summary
3. Paste it into **any free AI** for analysis:
   - ChatGPT
   - Claude  
   - Gemini
   - Grok
   - Perplexity

### Step 4: Export
Download your results as a CSV file for your own records.
