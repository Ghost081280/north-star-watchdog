## This Entire Site is Managed by AI

**Heads up:** This site runs on autopilot. Every hour, AI:
- Scans the news for Minnesota fraud stories
- Decides what's trending and what's breaking
- Adds new key figures when they appear in headlines
- Generates investigation angles and story ideas
- Writes the hourly briefing you see on the homepage
- Updates the stats and search suggestions

**We built it. AI runs it.** We have no idea what it's going to surface next - and that's kind of the point. The AI follows the story wherever it goes.

## How To Use It

### Step 1: Search
Type any name, organization, or program into the search box

### Step 2: View Database Results
We instantly search **real government databases**:
- Federal grants and contracts (USASpending.gov)
- Nonprofit tax filings (ProPublica)
- Campaign finance records (FEC.gov)
- Minnesota state databases (DHS, SOS, Courts)
- Federal exclusion lists (OIG, SAM.gov, OFAC)

### Step 3: Click "Deep Research + AI Analysis"
This is where it gets powerful. We automatically:
- Search YouTube, TikTok, X, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn
- Find recent news articles
- Provide links to DOJ, court records, and local news
- **Run AI analysis on everything we found**

### Step 4: Get Real AI Analysis
Our AI reads all your results and gives you:
- Key findings and what they mean
- Red flags and concerning patterns
- Connections to the broader scandal
- Recommended next steps

### Step 5: Continue Your Research
Copy the research summary and paste it into your favorite AI:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Grok
- Perplexity

### Step 6: Export
Download all your results as a CSV file for your own records.

## Setup

1. Fork this repository
2. Get a FREE Groq API key: https://console.groq.com/keys
3. Add to GitHub Secrets: Settings > Secrets > Actions
   - Name: `GROQ_API_KEY`
   - Value: `gsk_your_key_here`
4. Enable GitHub Pages: Settings > Pages > Source: main branch
5. The AI will start updating every hour automatically
