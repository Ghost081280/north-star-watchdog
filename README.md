## 🚀 How To Use It

### Step 1: Search
Type any name, organization, or program into the search box:
- `Feeding Our Future`
- `Tim Walz`
- `Aimee Bock`
- Or click any **Trending** topic

### Step 2: View Database Results
We instantly search **real government databases**:
- Federal grants & contracts (USASpending.gov)
- Nonprofit tax filings (ProPublica)
- Campaign finance records (FEC.gov)

### Step 3: Click "Deep Research + AI Analysis"
This is where it gets powerful. We automatically:
- Search YouTube, TikTok, and X for related videos
- Find recent news articles
- Provide links to DOJ, court records, and local news
- **Run AI analysis on everything we found**

### Step 4: Get Real AI Analysis
Our AI reads all your results and gives you:
- Key findings and what they mean
- Red flags and concerning patterns
- Connections to the broader scandal
- Recommended next steps

### Step 5: Continue Your Research
Want to dig deeper? Copy the research summary and paste it into your favorite AI:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Grok
- Perplexity

### Step 6: Export
Download all your results as a CSV file for your own records.
